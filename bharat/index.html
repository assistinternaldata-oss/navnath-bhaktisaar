॥ श्री ॥

    नाथ-वेश

    What the Naths carried

    ॥ अलख निरंजन ॥

    नवनाथ भारतवर्ष

    The ancient Bharat of the nine Naths

    Walk the land of the Navnath Bhaktisar: the forests, rivers and mountains the nine Naths crossed, the great temples standing whole, and the few things a Nath carried.

    This is a pictorial map: distances are drawn close, and a few places stand a little apart from where they truly are, so that each can be seen.
